## What this covers
- **The characters** Kiwi, Sofi and Smok Junior: their names as characters, looks, designs and personalities.
- **All art:** everything in `art/` and `assets/`, including the pixel art our scripts in `tools/` generate from it,
  and the placeholder art drawn in code (`levels/tilesets/`, textures baked in `src/scenes/art.ts`).
- **The logo** and the title "Maltipoo Sisters" as the name of this game.
- **The story and setting:** `docs/story-and-levels.md`, `docs/art-direction.md`, and the story and dialogue
  text in `src/i18n/locales/` (plain UI strings like "Settings" may be reused with the code).
- **The level designs:** the maps in `levels/*.tmj`.
